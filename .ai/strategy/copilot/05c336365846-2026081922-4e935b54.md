## Proposals

### PROP-001 — Cross Venue Net Arbitrage: IMPROVE (confidence 0.72)

**Chain scope:** SOLANA, EVM

The arbitrage hypothesis demands positive net edge after fees, slippage, price impact and latency reserve — this is sound. The structural gap is that `net_edge_bps` in `cross_chain_strategy_signals.py` is computed without any maximum `quote_age_ms` guard. A stale quote can inflate apparent edge on slower chains or during network congestion. A configurable quote-staleness threshold should be added to the shadow executor.

**Falsifiable shadow test:** Run SHADOW with and without a 500 ms quote_age_ms cap on both chains for ≥8 windows; compare net P&L and profit factor.

**Evidence needed before LIVE:** Positive profit factor (≥1.10) in ≥8 shadow windows on both SOLANA and EVM with the staleness cap active.

### PROP-002 — Liquidity Confirmed Momentum: SHADOW_MORE (confidence 0.65)

**Chain scope:** SOLANA, EVM

The hypothesis requires simultaneous confirmation from price acceleration, liquidity, and flow signals. All three input fields exist in `MarketFeatures` but no explicit multi-signal gate enforcing simultaneous positivity is visible in the shadow executor. A single strong signal could drive entries that the confirmation rule would reject.

**Falsifiable shadow test:** Shadow ≥10 windows; count windows where all three signals are simultaneously positive. If participation < 20% of eligible opportunities, diagnose the blocking filter.

**Evidence needed before escalation to IMPROVE:** Participation ≥20% of eligible windows and profit factor ≥1.10 in ≥10 shadow windows.

### PROP-003 — Dislocation Mean Reversion: SHADOW_MORE (confidence 0.68)

**Chain scope:** SOLANA, EVM

Mean reversion requires liquidity stability during the dislocation window, but no minimum `pool_age_seconds` is enforced. Very new pools can show large `dislocation_z` values that are structural (permanent repricing after a rug or launch event) rather than mean-reverting. Entering without a pool-age guard increases expected tail loss.

**Falsifiable shadow test:** Shadow ≥8 windows split by pool_age_seconds < 7200 vs ≥7200. Accept pool-age guard if young-pool subset has profit_factor < 1.0 with ≥5 trades.

**Evidence needed before escalation:** Confirmation that older-pool entries have profit_factor ≥1.10 across ≥8 windows.

### PROP-004 — Flow Acceleration: IMPROVE (confidence 0.74)

**Chain scope:** SOLANA, EVM

`avoid_single_wallet_dependency=True` is correctly set, but `independent_wallet_count` in `MarketFeatures` has no enforced minimum in the shadow executor. A single large wallet generating high `flow_acceleration_z` could bypass the concentration guard. A minimum of 3 independent wallets should be enforced at shadow entry.

**Falsifiable shadow test:** Shadow ≥8 windows with independent_wallet_count < 3 filtered out vs baseline. Confirm participation ≥20% of eligible opportunities.

**Evidence needed before LIVE:** Participation ≥20% and profit factor ≥1.10 in ≥8 windows with the count guard active.

### PROP-007 — Cross-Chain Cost Parity: RESEARCH_MORE (confidence 0.63)

**Chain scope:** SOLANA, EVM

All six families share the same chain-agnostic cost fields in `MarketFeatures`. Solana priority fees and rent differ structurally from EVM gas+MEV costs. Without chain-specific cost calibration evidence, `net_edge_bps` may be systematically over- or under-stated on one chain, leading to systematically higher entry rates and lower realised P&L on the miscalibrated chain.

**Research plan:**
- Use Etherscan API V2 to reconstruct actual gas+MEV costs for a sample of EVM shadow trades.
- Use Jupiter quote API to validate Solana priority-fee estimates.
- Compare to `fees_bps` recorded at entry.

**Falsifiable shadow test:** Compare shadow net P&L per executed basis-point of stated `net_edge_bps` separately for SOLANA and EVM entries across ≥10 windows. Investigate under-performance on either chain.

1. **No runtime evidence available.** All findings are structural. Profitability, canary readiness, and live readiness cannot be assessed without shadow P&L records.
2. **Governance structure is sound.** The three-agent contract, `validate_agent_report`, and the master policy gate correctly enforce SHADOW-only, confidence ≥0.85, ≥2 agent support, and bounded file allow-lists for any auto-code change.
