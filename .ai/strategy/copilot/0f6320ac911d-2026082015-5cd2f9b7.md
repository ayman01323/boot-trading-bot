## Proposals

### CSP-001 — Strengthen net-edge guard in `cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`

**Action:** IMPROVE | **Confidence:** 0.88 | **Risk:** LOW | **Chains:** SOLANA, EVM

`cross_chain_strategy_signals.py` computes `net_edge_bps` as gross edge minus all costs (fees, slippage, price impact, latency reserve). Downstream callers are not uniformly guarded by an explicit positive-net-edge check before treating the signal as actionable. A validated emit-signal helper that returns `None` or raises when `net_edge_bps <= 0` closes the gap without touching any live execution path.

**Evidence:** `learnerbot/cross_chain_strategy_signals.py` — `net_edge_bps` property; no caller-side positive-edge enforcement visible.

**Shadow test:** Feed `MarketFeatures` instances with net_edge_bps ≤ 0 into the proposed guard and assert signal is suppressed. Feed instances with net_edge_bps > threshold and assert they pass. Measure false-suppression rate against historical Solana and EVM shadow windows.

**Suggested files:** `learnerbot/cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`, `tests/test_cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`

---

### CSP-002 — Add chain-specific minimum `latency_reserve_bps` to `MarketFeatures`

**Action:** IMPROVE | **Confidence:** 0.85 | **Risk:** LOW | **Chains:** SOLANA, EVM

`MarketFeatures.latency_reserve_bps` defaults to `Decimal('0')` regardless of chain. On Solana, MEV competition means a nonzero latency reserve is warranted; on EVM, gas price variance and block-time uncertainty require a similar reserve. A chain-specific factory or post-init validation enforcing a minimum prevents silent cost underestimation in shadow windows.

**Evidence:** `learnerbot/cross_chain_strategy_signals.py` — `latency_reserve_bps` defaults to `Decimal('0')`.

**Shadow test:** Compare shadow-window net_profit distributions for SOLANA and EVM with latency_reserve_bps=0 vs a calibrated minimum. Accept if mean net_edge_bps decreases ≤ 15% and false-positive trade rate drops.

**Suggested files:** `learnerbot/cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`, `tests/test_cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`

---

### CSP-003 — Enforce `MIN_ELIGIBLE_OPPORTUNITIES` before PROMOTION_CANDIDATE transition

**Action:** IMPROVE | **Confidence:** 0.87 | **Risk:** LOW | **Chains:** SOLANA, EVM

`strategy_lab.py` defines `MIN_ELIGIBLE_OPPORTUNITIES=10` and `MIN_ELIGIBLE_PARTICIPATION=0.20`. The promotion path in `shadow_strategy_executor.py` should explicitly verify both before allowing a strategy to reach `PROMOTION_CANDIDATE` status. Without this gate, a strategy could be promoted on a small lucky sample.

**Evidence:** `learnerbot/strategy_lab.py` — thresholds defined; `learnerbot/shadow_strategy_executor.py` — promotion path.

**Shadow test:** Run SHADOW evaluation with eligible_opportunities < 10 and assert status never reaches `PROMOTION_CANDIDATE`. Run a second window meeting all thresholds and assert promotion is allowed.

**Suggested files:** `learnerbot/shadow_strategy_executor.py`, `tests/test_market_feature_shadow_executor.py`

### CSP-004 — RESEARCH_MORE: Multi-wallet cohort validation via Dune for route-fingerprint confidence formula

**Action:** RESEARCH_MORE | **Confidence:** 0.72 | **Risk:** LOW | **Chains:** SOLANA, EVM

`strategy.py` `learn_patterns` builds confidence scores from repeat_component + wallet_component + proof_component. This formula has not been validated against an independent on-chain multi-wallet cohort. A Dune query over publicly observed DEX wallets sharing the same route fingerprints would either corroborate or refute the formula.

**Evidence:** `learnerbot/strategy.py` — `learn_patterns` confidence formula.

**Research plan:**
1. Query Dune public datasets for wallets executing the same DEX route fingerprints observed by the bot on both Solana and EVM.
2. Extract gross_profit, gross_loss, fees_paid per wallet per route across at least 2 distinct time windows.
3. Compute external profit_factor and compare against bot-computed confidence scores.
4. Accept the formula if Spearman correlation > 0.6 out-of-sample.

**Research tools:** Dune, DEX Screener API, DefiLlama

**Suggested files:** `learnerbot/strategy_lab_research.py`
