## Executive Summary

Architecture review of source commit `154d417006d3461fa90b89b7c41f6a5cb5b6424d` (merged PR #163: _require positive follower executable edge_).

Runtime evidence is stale (>2 h); **profitability and live-readiness cannot be claimed for this cycle**. The review is based on static architecture inspection only.

PR #163 strengthened entry discipline on the Solana leader-copy strategy by requiring a positive follower round-trip net-edge estimate after Jupiter friction, network fees, slippage reserve and latency reserve before any new BUY is placed. This is a sound safety improvement. However, four structural gaps remain that limit durable net P&L:

1. The edge gate uses fixed constants uncalibrated against real fee distributions.
2. The Solana leader strategy has no per-position time-bounded or trailing-loss exit.
3. EVM coverage remains thin with no active liquidity-confirmed momentum strategy.
4. Strategy internal learning is scoped only to this bot's own trades; cohort-level pattern research is absent.

Four targeted proposals are included — two IMPROVE, one NEW_SHADOW and one RESEARCH_MORE — all SHADOW-bounded and requiring human approval before any live promotion.

### PROP-002 — Add time-bounded exit rule to Solana leader-copy strategy

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Action | IMPROVE |
| Chain scope | SOLANA |
| Confidence | 0.78 |

**Rationale:** The restored first-day Solana leader strategy has no explicit maximum hold-time exit or trailing-loss exit at the position level. Positions that fail to appreciate accumulate opportunity cost and increase exposure to liquidity deterioration. A time-bounded exit at T+90 min with a -8% trailing-loss floor reduces average loss magnitude while preserving upside.

**Evidence:**
- `learnerbot/solana_first_day_strategy_restore_patch.py` — no maximum hold-time defined
- `learnerbot/solana_exit_circuit_breaker_patch.py` — global circuit breaker, not per-position time limit

**Shadow test:** Shadow-test a 90-minute maximum hold with a -8% trailing-loss floor for 7 days. Accept if average loss per losing trade falls by ≥10% and profit factor remains above 1.1 across at least 20 closed positions.

**Suggested files:** `learnerbot/strategy_lab.py`, `learnerbot/shadow_strategy_executor.py`

**Research tools:** Dune wallet cohort hold-time vs return analysis
