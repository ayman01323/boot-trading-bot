## Summary

The reviewed Strategy Lab architecture is well-governed for report-only strategy work:

- new strategies remain SHADOW-only;
- cross-chain portability is explicitly gated per chain;
- first-party internal evidence (`INT1`, `INT2`) is prioritised ahead of external research;
- the hourly forensics export includes portfolio scorecards, shadow execution, and research context.

However, the source-commit review still supports two targeted improvements for more durable net-P&L assessment:

1. **Make portfolio evaluation more recency-aware.**  
   `learnerbot/strategy_lab.py` evaluates lifecycle status from lifetime-aggregated windows, and `learnerbot/strategy_lab_audit_patch.py` feeds that into the hourly report path. This can hide regime deterioration or recovery when older windows dominate.

2. **Expose freshness/dispersion metadata in research cohorts.**  
   `learnerbot/strategy_lab_research.py` and `learnerbot/strategy_internal_learning.py` provide strong anonymised cohort summaries, but the sanitised outputs do not consistently surface recency details for wallets, patterns, and behavior rankings. With stale runtime evidence, that weakens confidence calibration for new SHADOW hypotheses.
