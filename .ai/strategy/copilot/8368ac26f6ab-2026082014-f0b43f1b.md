## Architecture assessment

The Strategy Lab framework at this commit is correctly structured:

- All new strategies start in `SHADOW` by policy; promotion requires explicit evidence thresholds (`MIN_PROFIT_FACTOR=1.10`, `MIN_EVALUATION_TRADES=8`, `MIN_EVALUATION_WINDOWS=3`).
- The live execution path and the strategy research path are cleanly separated; `cross_chain_strategy_signals.py` and `shadow_strategy_executor.py` import no wallet, signer or RPC-submission code.
- The `three_agent_strategy_contract.py` policy gate enforces confidence ≥ 0.85, two independent supporting agents and a bounded file allow-list before any auto-code action can proceed.
- Cost fields (`fees_bps`, `slippage_bps`, `price_impact_bps`, `latency_reserve_bps`) exist in `MarketFeatures` and are subtracted from gross edge before a net-edge signal is emitted.

One architectural concern is raised (COPILOT-STRATEGY-001): the cost fields all default to `Decimal('0')`, and neither the signal evaluator nor the shadow executor currently verifies that realistic cost data was supplied before recording a window result. This means shadow window profit factors may be inflated if a feature adapter is not providing real cost data.

## Proposals

### COPILOT-STRATEGY-001 — Enforce non-optional cost accounting before signal emission

**Action:** IMPROVE  
**Chains:** SOLANA, EVM  
**Confidence:** 0.88

**Finding:** In `learnerbot/cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`, the `MarketFeatures` dataclass fields `fees_bps`, `slippage_bps`, `price_impact_bps` and `latency_reserve_bps` all default to `Decimal('0')`. A feature adapter that omits these fields produces a `net_edge = gross_edge - 0` result, making every non-zero gross-edge signal look profitable. The shadow executor in `shadow_strategy_executor.py` records `net_profit` from the net-edge output without independently checking whether cost fields were populated.

**Impact:** Shadow window profit factors and win rates may be materially overstated if cost data is zero-filled. Until this is verified, all shadow window results should be treated as potentially optimistic.

**Falsifiable shadow test:** After the improvement, pass a `MarketFeatures` instance with all cost fields set to zero and assert that the evaluator warns or rejects before emitting a positive-net-edge signal. Pass realistic costs (`fees_bps=30`, `slippage_bps=20`) and assert `net_edge = gross_edge - 50 bps`. Add these assertions to `tests/test_cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`.

**Runtime evidence needed:**
- Shadow window records showing actual `fees_bps` and `slippage_bps` values vs zero in `strategy_lab_windows.metadata_json`.
- Execution log comparing actual gas/fee paid with what was recorded in `net_profit` for at least 20 shadow trades.

**Suggested files:** `learnerbot/cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`, `learnerbot/shadow_strategy_executor.py`, `tests/test_cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`

**Chains:** SOLANA, EVM

**Hypothesis:** Tokens exhibiting simultaneous positive flow acceleration (`flow_acceleration_z > 1.0`) and positive price momentum (`momentum_z > 1.5`) on a pool with adequate liquidity (`liquidity_score > 0.6`) will produce positive net P&L after all fees, slippage and price-impact costs, with a profit factor ≥ 1.10 over at least 8 shadow trades and 3 evaluation windows.

**Differentiation from leader copy:** Signal is derived entirely from the bot's own `MarketFeatures` data stream, not from any individual wallet's behaviour. Combines three independent quality dimensions (flow acceleration, momentum, liquidity) rather than copying a single wallet's entry.

**Falsification conditions:**
- Shadow profit factor < 1.0 over 8+ trades on either chain type.
- Average net P&L after costs < 0 in the first evaluation window.

Evaluate Solana and EVM windows independently.

2. Query DEX Screener API for pools matching the signal thresholds; compute hypothetical net edge after 30 bps fees and 20 bps slippage.
3. Validate with Jupiter for Solana route cost data before running shadow.

**Research tools:** Dune, DEX Screener API, Jupiter

### COPILOT-STRATEGY-003 — Mean-reversion/dislocation SHADOW strategy (|dislocation_z| > 2.0 with quality gates)

**Action:** NEW_SHADOW  
**Chains:** SOLANA, EVM  
**Confidence:** 0.76

**Hypothesis:** Tokens/pools exhibiting statistically significant price dislocation (`|dislocation_z| > 2.0`) with adequate liquidity (`liquidity_score > 0.5`) and route replicability (`route_replicability > 0.5`) will revert towards the mean within the observation window, producing positive net P&L after all costs. This holds independently on Solana and EVM but requires chain-specific cost calibration.

**Differentiation from leader copy:** Derived from the `dislocation_z` signal in `MarketFeatures`, which is computed from the bot's own market data. Not dependent on any single wallet's behaviour. Two-sided (can act on positive or negative dislocation) and diversifies away from momentum strategies.

**Falsification conditions:**
- Largest loss in any shadow window exceeds 3× the average net gain per trade.
- Shadow profit factor < 1.0 over 8+ trades.
- Dislocation events do not reverse within the expected horizon in more than 50% of cases.

**Key risk:** Mean-reversion can fail catastrophically on low-liquidity or rug-adjacent pools. The `liquidity_score` and `route_replicability` gates are critical and must be validated before running shadow trades.

**Falsifiable shadow test:** Register with `family=MEAN_REVERSION`, `source=AI_PROPOSED`. Assert `|dislocation_z| > 2.0` at entry for every recorded shadow trade. Assert `profit_factor ≥ 1.10` and `largest_loss < 3 × average_net_gain` across all shadow windows. Evaluate Solana and EVM independently.

**Research plan:**
1. Use DEX Screener API to identify historical dislocation events (price deviation > 2σ from 1h VWAP) for liquid pools.
2. Compute hypothetical net P&L for mean-reversion entries at dislocation peak with realistic costs.
3. Use DefiLlama to confirm TVL stability during dislocation events (avoid dislocation caused by rug/exit liquidity).

**Research tools:** DEX Screener API, DefiLlama, Dune

**Runtime evidence needed:** Shadow window results after at least 3 evaluation windows on each chain; historical dislocation frequency and post-event reversion magnitude from DEX Screener API.

**Suggested files:** `learnerbot/strategy_ai_proposals.py`, `learnerbot/cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`, `tests/test_cross_chain_strategy_signals.py`
