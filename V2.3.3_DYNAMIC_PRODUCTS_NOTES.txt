BOOT v2.3.3 — Dynamic AUTO Product Universe

Purpose
-------
Expand the current V2/V3 market scanner beyond a tiny fixed seed list while keeping
execution fail-closed. Products are learned automatically from the bot's verified
V2 and V3 pool registries. No external token-list API is required.

Risk levels
-----------
L1 CORE
  Wrapped native assets and configured stablecoins. Highest priority.

L2 LIQUID
  Operator-configured liquid seeds and discovered products with multiple verified
  pools, a wrapped-base connection and sufficient observation age.

L3 STRICT / SHADOW
  New or less-established discovered products. Very new products remain SHADOW.
  Products with enough verified connectivity may become STRICT AUTO, but use a
  tighter price-impact ceiling and still require wallet simulation and final eth_call.

L4 QUARANTINE
  Products under the execution-mismatch quarantine are excluded from scanning and AUTO.

Telegram
--------
/products   Show current product universe, risk level and AUTO/SHADOW/BLOCK status.
/power      Shows product totals alongside V2/V3/cross-DEX scanner status.

Execution controls retained
---------------------------
* exact current quote
* product risk/policy gate
* price-impact/liquidity gate
* wallet-specific simulation
* gas-inclusive retained-profit test
* final immediate eth_call preflight
* bounded capital/allowance controls
* execution-mismatch quarantine
* cross-DEX remains SHADOW until an atomic multi-router executor is deployed

The product classifier is not a claim that a token is safe or valuable. It is a local
routing/risk prioritisation layer. Actual execution still fails closed if the current
route cannot be reproduced safely and profitably for the user's wallet.
