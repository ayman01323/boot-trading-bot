## KEEP — Solana fail-closed Strategy Lab evaluation

## REWORK — Cross-chain executable-edge gating
Positive executable edge must exceed chain-, route-, size- and congestion-specific cost uncertainty. Solana requires priority-fee, account/rent and landing-risk reserves; EVM requires current EIP-1559 gas, builder/private-order-flow costs, revert probability and replacement costs.

## IMPROVE — Realized outcome and failure attribution
STRATEGY/MARKET loss means a successfully executable position lost after complete costs. EXECUTION/INFRASTRUCTURE failure means quote expiry, simulation rejection, revert, non-landing, RPC failure, reconciliation failure or inability to exit. Combining them obscures whether selection or delivery should change.
