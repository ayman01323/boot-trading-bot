# GPT strategy review

Architecture-only review completed. The repository contains meaningful fail-closed execution protections, but no fresh runtime evidence supports profitability or promotion. Solana leader-copy losses must be classified as STRATEGY/MARKET when transactions execute correctly but cost-adjusted exits lose money; landed-invalid transactions, quote/RPC failures and blocked exits are EXECUTION/INFRASTRUCTURE failures. EVM atomic cycles have wallet-specific gas simulation and atomic minimum-output protection, while EVM leader-copy entry validation does not establish positive forward net edge. Solana SHADOW features deliberately lack current executable edge, so Solana strategy claims remain unproven.

## IMPROVE — Cross-chain Strategy Lab scorecards
Durable money-weighted decisions require time-aligned out-of-sample outcomes net of all costs and failed attempts, not signal counts or simulated win frequency.

## SHADOW_MORE — Solana leader-copy positive-edge entry
Historical leader returns and defensive execution limits do not prove positive follower edge at the follower's delayed entry, size and exit path.

## REWORK — EVM SiBot leader-copy entry selection
A sellable token with bounded round-trip loss can still have negative expected follower P&L. Copy selection should be researched against executable forward edge rather than historical wallet rank or win count.

## KEEP — EVM atomic cycle execution protection
These are appropriate execution safeguards and should not be weakened, although architecture alone does not demonstrate profitable opportunity frequency.
