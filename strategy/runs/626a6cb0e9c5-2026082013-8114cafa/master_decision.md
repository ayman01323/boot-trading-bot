# GPT Master strategy decision

Architecture-only evidence supports bounded improvements to SHADOW cost accounting, trade-level attribution, and out-of-sample promotion evaluation. No profitability, CANARY, LIVE, increased-frequency, or increased-capital conclusion is supported. Proposed liquidity strategies and participation auditing duplicate checked-in Strategy Lab capabilities, while the proposed copy-entry forecast duplicates existing positive-edge gates and the Forecasted Positive Net Edge evaluator.

## REJECT — SiBot leader-copy entry
The cited base validators are not the complete checked-out control stack. EVM's profit-guard wrapper requires historical expected edge to cover estimated gas and round-trip costs, while Solana installs historical-return, realised-profit-factor, and loss-quarantine gates. Strategy Lab also already contains a Forecasted Positive Net Edge evaluator requiring positive current executable edge. The proposal is therefore substantially duplicative, and no runtime evidence demonstrates an additional forecast would improve net outcomes.

## ACCEPT — Solana SiBot SHADOW accounting
Code truth confirms SHADOW P&L uses fixed entry and exit fee defaults despite the execution stack modelling additional priority, tip, platform, impact, failure, and refundable-rent components. Recording observed cost components and separately reporting stress imputations is justified without asserting profitability.

## ACCEPT — Cross-chain Strategy Lab evaluation
Strategy Lab stores aggregate windows and counts execution failures, but lacks trade-level capital duration, incurred failure cost, wallet-delta reconciliation, and mutually exclusive outcome attribution. A SHADOW-only ledger would strengthen evidence quality without changing trading behavior.

## ACCEPT — Strategy lifecycle promotion evaluation
The checked-out Strategy Lab can label a promotion candidate from three aggregate windows, eight trades, positive net P&L, and profit factor at least 1.10 without mechanically enforcing a time-split holdout, uncertainty bound, chain separation, or regime coverage. Acceptance is limited to SHADOW promotion-candidate evaluation; the live CANARY state machine is outside the allowed scope and requires separate human review.

## REJECT — New Liquidity Quality and liquidity-confirmed momentum
The repository already seeds both Liquidity Confirmed Momentum and New Liquidity Quality and implements a cross-chain new-liquidity evaluator with sellability, liquidity, dispersion, freshness, and positive-net-edge gates. The proposed age, price-change, depth, stop, target, fee, and MEV constants have no measured runtime support; Copilot's claimed exploitable mean-reversion window is unsupported and internally mismatched with a momentum entry.

## REJECT — Shadow strategy participation-rate audit
Strategy Lab already records eligible opportunities, trades, participation, and signal skips, and evaluate_strategy already returns REWORK_FILTERS when participation is below its configured threshold. Treating low participation as evidence that safety filters should be loosened would risk forced trading; absent opportunity-level outcomes, abstention may be correct.
