# Claude strategy review

Architecture-only review (no fresh runtime forensics). The bot runs three parallel strategy families -- EVM triangular/multi-hop arbitrage (route_scanner.py/market_scanner.py/live_executor.py), directional leader-copy trading on both chains (sibot.py/solana_sibot.py), and a governed, non-signing market-native Strategy Lab (cross_chain_strategy_signals.py/shadow_strategy_executor.py/strategy_canary.py) -- all sharing multi-layer pre-trade profitability, slippage, price-impact, liquidity and sellability gates. The safety architecture (execution quarantine, exit circuit breakers, platform profit-factor throttle, AI-council + staged canary promotion) is comparatively mature. The main gaps found are EXECUTION/INFRASTRUCTURE rather than strategy-signal issues: EVM arbitrage cycles broadcast via public mempool with no MEV/private-relay protection despite razor-thin (3-6bps) required edges; Solana strategy-level P&L bookkeeping uses a flat fee estimate that may diverge from the dynamic priority fee actually paid and recorded elsewhere; EVM has no nonce-submission lock or gas-bump/RBF path and no wallet-level execution-fault kill switch analogous to Solana's; and a recent platform-wide Solana loss halt (2026-08-18) conflated realized loss with exit-retry/liquidity-gate behavior in its own commit message, which is exactly the strategy-vs-execution conflation this review is asked to avoid. No claim of measured live profitability or canary readiness is made given the missing runtime evidence.

## SHADOW_MORE — EVM triangular/multi-hop cycle arbitrage (route_scanner.py / market_scanner.py, executed via live_executor.py execute_cycle/execute_v3_cycle)
Atomic arbitrage cycles are high-value MEV targets because the entire edge is visible and copyable from the public mempool. With no private-relay/bundle path, a competing searcher can extract the discovered edge before it lands -- an EXECUTION/INFRASTRUCTURE cost that would look like 'strategy stopped working' unless measured separately from signal quality.

## IMPROVE — Solana SiMo copy-trading P&L accounting (solana_sibot.py) reconciled against live execution fee receipts (solana_execution_efficiency_patch.py)
Strategy-level realized P&L used for stop-loss/take-profit/canary-promotion decisions appears to subtract a flat conservative fee estimate rather than the dynamic priority fee actually paid and already recorded elsewhere. During Solana priority-fee spikes this understates true cost and can overstate net edge, misclassifying EXECUTION cost as STRATEGY profit and risking premature canary promotion on an inflated number.

## RESEARCH_MORE — EVM live execution transaction submission serialization (live_executor.py), shared across AUTO arbitrage scanner, SiMo copy engine, and manual commands
Multiple independent live-trading entry points exist per EVM wallet (AUTO scanner, SiMo copy engine, manual commands). Without a serialization lock, near-simultaneous nonce fetches can collide, producing a dropped/stuck transaction that surfaces as a failed execution rather than a bad strategy signal; with no gas-bump path, a transaction stuck under a rising base fee simply times out instead of completing, under-executing otherwise-profitable opportunities in a way that would misleadingly look like poor strategy performance unless separated out.

## RESEARCH_MORE — Solana stop-loss / emergency-exit root-cause attribution (solana_sibot.py stop-loss, solana_emergency_liquidity_unwind_patch.py, solana_emergency_loss_halt_migration.py)
A recent platform-wide halt explicitly conflated 'loss' with 'exit-retry pattern' in its own migration comment. If stop-losses were being delayed/blocked by the liquidity gate rather than price movement being the primary driver, the realized loss and the halt decision are actually an EXECUTION/INFRASTRUCTURE finding, not evidence the strategy's edge went negative -- these require different fixes and the current incident record does not distinguish them.

## KEEP — Cross-chain market-native strategy family (cross_chain_strategy_signals.py, 7 rule-based strategies) under shadow_strategy_executor.py, gated by strategy_canary.py AI-council promotion
This is architecturally sound: non-leader-dependent (addressing docs/STRATEGY_LAB.md's stated concern that leader-copying alone is an insufficient strategy portfolio), enforces a conservative common cost/liquidity/sellability floor, is explicitly non-signing while in SHADOW, and requires independent multi-agent agreement plus a small staged canary before any live capital is committed. No architectural weakening found.

## IMPROVE — EVM live-trading execution-fault circuit breaker (no account-level auto-disable equivalent to Solana's solana_execution_fault_counter_patch.py)
Solana has a dedicated per-user auto-disable safety net for ambiguous/invalid execution outcomes, distinct from strategy loss; no equivalent was found for EVM. This is a chain-symmetry gap in the execution/infra safety net: repeated EVM execution-mismatch faults (e.g. from an RPC provider issue or a router/ABI change) would only quarantine the specific route/token, not pause the account's EVM live trading the way Solana does.
