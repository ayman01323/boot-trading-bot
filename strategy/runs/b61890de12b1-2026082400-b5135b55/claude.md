# Claude strategy review

Evidence this cycle is architecture-only (.strategy_claude/evidence.json is a MISSING_RUNTIME_FORENSICS stub with generated_epoch=0); no live P&L, trade-latency, or infrastructure telemetry was available, so no measured profitability, win-rate, or canary-readiness claim is made. Code review confirms a mature, gated SHADOW->CANARY->LIVE promotion pipeline (strategy_lab.py, strategy_canary.py) and layered chain-specific execution-cost/sellability controls (EVM: gas-bid multiplier + profit-protected gas reserve + roundtrip rug gate; Solana: dynamic fee cap + reverse-quote liquidity preflight + rug/pool risk gate). Two architecture-level EXECUTION/INFRASTRUCTURE gaps are flagged as falsifiable SHADOW/observability hypotheses rather than strategy changes: (1) EVM has no found replace-by-fee/stuck-pending-tx monitor beyond a static gas-bid multiplier, a plausible source of missed exits during gas spikes; (2) Solana's ambiguous-exit circuit breaker has a manual-recovery precedent (the 8fip write-off) but no found automated metric for open live_exit_circuit backlog/age, so operator-visible EXECUTION-failure cost cannot currently be distinguished from STRATEGY/MARKET loss without new observability. A third item questions whether the shared min_net_bps entry gate in cross_chain_strategy_signals.py adequately reflects EVM's higher gas-cost variance versus Solana's more predictable fee structure. All three proposals are read-only-observability or SHADOW-only in scope; none touch LIVE/risk/capital/signing.

## RESEARCH_MORE — EVM stuck-pending-transaction / replace-by-fee observability
If a submitted EVM tx becomes underpriced mid-flight (gas spike after submission), it can sit pending and block the account's subsequent nonces, delaying or preventing a timed exit. This is an EXECUTION/INFRASTRUCTURE risk distinct from strategy signal quality, and current mitigations (RPC failover, static gas-bid multiplier) do not address it. This is inferred from the absence of replace-by-fee code in the explored files, not from a confirmed incident — no runtime evidence was available this cycle to confirm frequency.

## RESEARCH_MORE — Solana ambiguous-exit circuit-breaker backlog/age metric
The combination of a per-user circuit breaker, a documented emergency all-user halt, and at least one manual write-off suggests ambiguous/failed Solana exits are a recurring EXECUTION/INFRASTRUCTURE cost, but the architecture has no found automated visibility into how many positions are currently stuck behind an open circuit or how long reconciliation takes. Per this review's mandate to separate STRATEGY/MARKET loss from EXECUTION/INFRASTRUCTURE failure, this gap is currently unmeasured, not necessarily unmanaged.

## RESEARCH_MORE — Chain-specific (vs. shared) minimum net-edge threshold in common signal gate
A single shared min_net_bps entry threshold may not correctly price in that EVM cost-to-trade can spike disproportionately during congestion (via the gas-bid multiplier) relative to Solana's typically smaller, more bounded fee structure. This could mean the same nominal edge threshold is more permissive on EVM during congestion (net loss after real gas) or unnecessarily conservative on Solana in calm periods (missed otherwise-profitable trades). This is a hypothesis for further research, not a confirmed defect — the review agents did not verify whether min_net_bps is already parameterized per chain in configuration (evidence gap).
