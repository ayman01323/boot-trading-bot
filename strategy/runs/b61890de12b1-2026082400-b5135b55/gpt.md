# GPT strategy review

Architecture-only review completed. Safety separation and positive-edge gates are directionally sound, but profitability cannot be evaluated because fresh runtime forensics are absent. Cross-chain Strategy Lab aggregation can combine incomparable native-unit amounts, Solana SHADOW lacks executable quote/outcome economics, and EVM SHADOW records quote simulations rather than failure-adjusted realised outcomes. These are evidence/accounting limitations, not proof of strategy or market loss. No strategy is canary-ready or live-ready on this evidence.

## REWORK — Strategy Lab portfolio evaluation
Money-weighted portfolio economics require a common valuation unit or strictly separated chain scorecards. Solana priority/rent economics and EVM gas/revert economics must not be combined as raw native quantities.

## NEW_SHADOW — Solana market-native and leader-copy hypotheses
Historical leader returns are not contemporaneous executable follower edge. A SHADOW adapter should measure both entry and exit economics using current size-specific routes, priority fees, account/rent effects, latency reserve and failure probability.

## IMPROVE — Cross Venue Net Arbitrage and Learned Route Replication
Passed simulations do not measure inclusion failures, reverts, stale-state losses, replacement fees or realised price impact. Durable edge must include the probability-weighted cost of failed attempts.

## SHADOW_MORE — All Strategy Lab hypotheses
Eight observations and aggregate profit factor do not establish durable edge across regimes, sizes or chains. Promotion should require independent forward evidence, uncertainty bounds and failure-adjusted net returns.
